passtra helps you discover events and places around you. We designed it to work with as little personal data as possible: you can use the app without an account.

## 1. What we process and why

| Data | When | Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Approximate location** (rounded to ~100 m) | Only while you use the app and only if you allow it | To show what is near you and calculate distances | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); you can withdraw it in system settings | **Not stored.** Used to answer each request, then discarded. We do not keep location history |
| City you choose | When you pick a city | Explore the City | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) | On your device; in your account if signed in |
| Preferences (language, interests, budget, optional age range) | When you set them | Personalising suggestions; hiding 18+ content if you choose an age range | Contract / consent | Until you change them or delete your account |
| Account e-mail | Only if you sign in | Sign-in with a one-time code, syncing saved items | Contract | Until you delete your account |
| Saved items and lists | When you save something | Your saved list | Contract | Until you remove them or delete your account |
| Activity in the app (opened, saved, shared, hidden items) | Only if signed in **and** "Personalised suggestions" is on | Better recommendations | Consent (you can switch it off in Privacy & data; switching off deletes this history) | 180 days |
| Push token | Only if you enable notifications | Sending the notifications you asked for | Consent | Until you disable notifications or delete your account |
| Reports and event submissions | When you send them | Correcting data, publishing organisers' events | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): accurate information | Until resolved + 12 months; your account link is removed if you delete your account |
| Technical logs (IP address, device type, errors) | Every request | Security, abuse prevention, rate limiting, troubleshooting | Legitimate interest | 30 days; IPs for anonymous reports are stored only as a salted hash |
| Search queries | When you search | Answering the search; queries are cached **without** any user identifier to reduce cost and latency | Legitimate interest | 30 days |

We do **not** sell personal data, do not use it for third-party advertising, and do not track you across other apps or websites.

## 2. Service providers (processors)
- Hosting and database: [hosting provider], EU region.
- Authentication: Supabase (e-mail sign-in) — [region].
- Push notifications: Expo (Expo push service) and Apple/Google push services.
- Search interpretation (only if enabled): Anthropic — receives the text of your search query and UI language, never your identity or location.
- Map display: Apple Maps (iOS) or Google Maps SDK (Android) render the map on your device.
- Place details "From Google": when you open a place, our server requests current details from Google Places; your identity and location are not sent.

International transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

## 3. Your rights
Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent. In the app: **Profile → Privacy & data → Download my data / Delete my account** (deletion is immediate and permanent). You can also e-mail privacy@passtra.app and lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy).

## 4. Children
passtra is not directed at children under 13. If you indicate you are under 18, 18+ venues and events (e.g. nightclubs, casinos) are hidden.

## 5. Data sources and accuracy
Event and place information comes from public and official sources (for example the Cyprus Deputy Ministry of Tourism calendar, OpenStreetMap contributors, Wikidata/Wikimedia Commons, MET Norway) and from organisers. Each item shows its sources and when it was last checked. It may still be out of date — please check with the organiser before you go.

## 6. Changes
We will notify you in the app of material changes to this policy.
